OUR STORY

A Legacy Built on
Quality & Trust

Shreekamal Oil Industries carries forward a family legacy that began in 1969 in cattle feed and oil trading. What started as a small trading business has grown — through decades of honest dealing and uncompromising quality — into one of Central India's trusted cattle feed producers.

1969 — THE BEGINNING
Our family begins its journey in Cotton Seed Oil Cake & Oil Trading — laying the foundation of trust and quality that would define the group for generations.
2011 — SHREEKAMAL OIL INDUSTRIES FOUNDED
Building on 40+ years of legacy, Shreekamal Agro is formally established at Hinganghat, Maharashtra — scaling operations with modern milling technology.
2015–2020 — EXPANSION ACROSS INDIA
Group expands operations — Aadit Oil Mill and Singhal Agro Products established. Network grows to 5+ states with multiple brand launches including Radha Govind, Panchratan & Radha Kaithal.
TODAY — TRUSTED ACROSS 7 STATES
5 Lakh+ happy clients across 7 states. Consistent growth through quality products and timely deliveries — and the journey continues.
