OUR STORY

A Legacy of Purity,
Trust & Growth

1969 — THE BEGINNING
Our family begins its journey in Cotton Seed Oil Cake & Cattle Feed Trading — laying the foundation of trust and quality that would define the group for generations.
2011 — SHREEKAMAL GROUP FOUNDED
Shreekamal Oil Industries is formally established at Hinganghat, Maharashtra — scaling operations with modern milling technology.
2015 – ONGOING — EXPANSION ACROSS INDIA
Network grows to 5+ states with multiple brand launches including Radha Govind & Panchratan. Today, 5 Lakh+ happy clients across 7 states — consistent growth through quality products and timely deliveries, and the journey continues.
